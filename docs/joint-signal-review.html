Joint signals in traj_compare

See actual samples under the mouse, inspect signed limits, and open a run’s recording from the report.

Inspecting the traces

  • Open Raw signals per joint for standalone q, qd, qdd, qddd and T, or combine them in q / qd / qdd / qddd or q / qd / T.
  • Hover to read the nearest visible sample, with time and units. Use Limits on a standalone plot to inspect its signed bounds.
  • The fixture supplies an independent shoulder gearbox load and omits the slide gearbox load. Its shoulder bounds are ±16 N·m command, ±17 N·m applied and ±18 N·m gearbox load.

What the torque curves mean

Required
Inverse-dynamics torque supplied for a target.
Commanded
The controller request, including any summed effort before saturation if that is what the source logged.
Applied
Delivered actuator effort reported by the source.
Gearbox load
Transmission load explicitly measured or computed by the supplied model. The plotted limit comes from that channel’s configured envelope.

Rerun

Open in Rerun uses the recording already on disk. The local report server can build a missing recording from the selected comparison and open it. A report opened from disk gives a copyable traj-compare-rerun command for running those steps locally.
